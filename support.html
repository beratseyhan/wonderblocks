How do I play?

Tap a block and it slides out in the direction of its arrow, if its path is clear. Drag to turn the landmark and pinch to zoom. Clear every block to bring the landmark down and travel on to the next destination.

How do I remove the ads?

Open the pause menu (or the gear on the World Tour map), then Settings. “Remove ads” is a one-time purchase that removes banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded hint videos stay available.

I bought “Remove ads” on another device.

Open Settings and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings and use the arrows next to “Language”. The game follows your device language the first time it starts.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There is no account. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted with it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings and tap “Ad privacy” (shown where Google’s consent message applies). On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking permission in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Something does not work.

Write to beratseyhan@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.
