Who we are

WonderBlocks is developed by Berat Seyhan. The Google Play publisher is App Atölyesi. For privacy questions and requests, contact beratseyhan@icloud.com.

In short

No account or sign-in is required. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. Advertising uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with Google AdMob, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads on Android; the available network may vary by ad and version. Purchases are processed by Google Play on Android or Apple on iOS and managed through RevenueCat. Tenjin measures installs and campaign performance. We do not receive your payment card details and do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars, best times, settings (language, music and sound volume, vibration), which tutorials you have seen, and whether you own “Remove ads”, and your coin and booster balances. This game progress stays on your device and is not sent to us. It may be included in your device’s own backup, such as iCloud Backup or Android backup, depending on your settings; clearing the app’s local data or deleting the app removes the on-device copy.

Advertising (Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay)

The free game may show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. On Android, Unity LevelPlay mediates Google AdMob, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. These providers may collect or share an Android Advertising ID (or, on iOS, an advertising identifier where permitted), IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad impressions and interactions, device and operating-system details, and diagnostics. They use these data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted by your choices, personalise ads. You can adjust ad privacy choices in the game's Settings, where available. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission also applies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase

On Android, purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; on iOS, by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The game offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase and consumable coin and booster packs. RevenueCat validates purchases and entitlements using an app user identifier, transaction and purchase history, and basic device and app information. We do not receive your payment card details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Measuring our own ads (Tenjin)

Tenjin measures where installations and purchases come from and how our campaigns perform. Depending on your platform and permissions, it may process installation and app-launch events, game interactions and milestones, purchase events and ad revenue, an installation identifier, advertising identifier, IP-derived approximate location, app version and device information. Campaign attribution results may be shared with advertising partners. On iOS, Apple's tracking permission applies. See the Tenjin Privacy Policy.

Game engine

WonderBlocks is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive anonymous technical information about the device the game runs on (such as device model, operating system, processor, graphics hardware and screen size) to improve the engine. It contains no game progress and nothing you enter. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

WonderBlocks is a game for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can change available ad privacy choices in the game's Settings and reset or delete the Android Advertising ID in your device's Google privacy and ads settings (the menu name varies by Android version). This changes the identifier available to future ad requests; it does not automatically erase data already processed by ad partners. For access or deletion requests concerning data held by us or our providers, email beratseyhan@icloud.com; we will help route requests to the relevant provider where necessary. You can also use the providers' privacy controls linked above. Game progress stored locally can be removed by deleting the app's local data.

Data retention, deletion and security

We keep no separate player account or copy of your local game progress. Your on-device game data remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall it; a device backup may persist under your backup provider's settings. Advertising, attribution and purchase providers retain data under their own linked privacy policies and applicable legal obligations. Email us to request access or deletion of data associated with your app use; we will process data we control and help direct requests to the relevant providers. Deleting the Android Advertising ID limits its use in future requests but does not by itself delete older provider records. Data sent by the game and its service SDKs to providers is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. We do not receive payment card details.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date below will change.
