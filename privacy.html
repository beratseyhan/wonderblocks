Who we are

WonderBlocks is made by Berat Seyhan, an independent developer. Contact: beratseyhan@icloud.com.

In short

No account and no sign-in. Your progress and settings stay on your device. Ads are provided by Unity Ads through Unity LevelPlay (version 1.0.1 and later; version 1.0 used Google AdMob). Purchases are handled by Apple and verified through RevenueCat. Installs, play milestones, ad revenue and purchases are measured by Tenjin so we can tell which of our own ads brought players to the game. We run no servers, use no crash-reporting service of our own and do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars, best times, settings (language, music and sound volume, vibration), which tutorials you have seen, and whether you own “Remove ads”. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. It can be part of your device’s own backup (for example iCloud Backup); deleting the app deletes it from the device.

Advertising (Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay)

The free game shows a banner ad on the map and between screens, interstitial ads at some level transitions (never on the first levels, and with a minimum gap between them), and rewarded videos that you can choose to watch for a reward. From version 1.0.1 the ads are served by Unity Ads, delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies and its ironSource subsidiary). These services may collect and process: your device’s advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking) and the identifier for vendors, IP address and the approximate (country-level) location derived from it, information about the ads shown and your interactions with them (views, taps, completed videos), device and operating-system information (such as model, language, screen size and connection type), SDK performance data (such as initialisation speed), and, if you report an ad, the content of that report. They use this to deliver, measure and optimise ads, to prevent fraud and to meet legal obligations; ads are personalised only where you have agreed or not objected (see below).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your choice before any ad is requested: “Allow personalised ads” or “Non-personalised ads only”. Elsewhere the same choice is available at any time and works as an opt-out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information under US state privacy laws. You can change it at any time in the game’s Settings under “Ad privacy”. On iPhone and iPad, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether WonderBlocks may track you; if you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared.

Version 1.0 of the game used Google AdMob for the same purpose; it stops being used when you update.

More: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy · Unity Privacy Policy (also covers ironSource).

In-app purchase

The one-time “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details. To switch the ads off and to restore the purchase on another device, the game verifies it through RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a purchase-validation service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID created by the game, the App Store transaction details of the purchase, and basic technical information (such as app version, operating-system version, device model, language and IP address). It does not receive your name, email or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Measuring our own ads (Tenjin)

To learn which of our advertising campaigns (for example on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok) brought a player to WonderBlocks, and whether those campaigns pay for themselves, the game uses Tenjin (Tenjin, Inc.), a mobile attribution service. Tenjin receives: the time of the install and of each app launch, a random installation identifier, your device’s advertising identifier only if you allow tracking in Apple’s prompt (otherwise a zero value), the identifier for vendors, IP address and the approximate country derived from it, device model, operating-system version, app version and language, a few play milestones (the tutorial finished, levels completed, the shop shown, rewarded videos watched), the estimated revenue of each ad shown to you, and the App Store transaction details of the “Remove ads” purchase (product, price, currency, transaction identifier and the App Store receipt, which contains no payment details). Tenjin matches installs with the ad networks’ click and view data and with Apple’s SKAdNetwork (which reports attributions without identifying you), and it may share aggregated or event-level results with the ad network that ran the campaign so it can measure and optimise our ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Tenjin only runs when you chose “Allow personalised ads” in the game’s ad privacy question, and everywhere it stops when you choose “Non-personalised ads only”; you can change that choice under “Ad privacy” in Settings. Declining tracking in Apple’s prompt keeps the advertising identifier out of all of this. See the Tenjin Privacy Policy.

Game engine

WonderBlocks is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive anonymous technical information about the device the game runs on (such as device model, operating system, processor, graphics hardware and screen size) to improve the engine. It contains no game progress and nothing you enter. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

WonderBlocks is a game for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data processed by Unity for advertising, use the “Ad privacy” option in the game, the privacy option shown inside Unity ads, the Unity links above, or your device’s tracking and advertising settings. You can contact us at any time with questions.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date below will change.
