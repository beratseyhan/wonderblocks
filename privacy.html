Who we are

WonderBlocks is made by Berat Seyhan, an independent developer. Contact: beratseyhan@icloud.com.

In short

No account and no sign-in. Your progress and settings stay on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Purchases are handled by Apple. We run no servers, use no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own and do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars, best times, settings (language, music and sound volume, vibration), which tutorials you have seen, and whether you own “Remove ads”. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. It can be part of your device’s own backup (for example iCloud Backup); deleting the app deletes it from the device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free game shows banner ads, interstitial ads between levels, and rewarded videos that you can choose to watch for a hint. The ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and process: your device’s advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, information about the ads shown and your interactions with them, device and operating-system information, and diagnostic data. Google uses this to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud; ads are personalised only where you have agreed. The game only requests ads rated for general audiences.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and where US state privacy laws require it, Google’s consent message asks for your choices before ads are requested. You can change them at any time in the game’s Settings under “Ad privacy”. On iPhone and iPad, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether WonderBlocks may track you; if you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase

The one-time “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details. The game checks the purchase on your device (Unity In-App Purchasing) to switch the ads off; no purchase data is sent to us.

Game engine

WonderBlocks is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive anonymous technical information about the device the game runs on (such as device model, operating system, processor, graphics hardware and screen size) to improve the engine. It contains no game progress and nothing you enter. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

WonderBlocks is a game for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the “Ad privacy” option in the game, Google’s controls linked above, or your device’s tracking and advertising settings. You can contact us at any time with questions.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date below will change.
